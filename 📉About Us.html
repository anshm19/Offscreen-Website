We created this website to help people understand how much time they spend on their phones, tablets, and computers each day. Many people do not realize how quickly minutes turn into hours when watching videos, playing games, or browsing social media. Our goal is to provide a clear and simple tool that helps users track their daily screen habits so they can stay focused on schoolwork, hobbies, and offline activities.

This project started with a common frustration: automatic screen time limits are far too easy to ignore. When a phone tells you that your time is up, it takes less than a second to dismiss the alert and keep scrolling without a second thought. We wanted to create a system that puts the control back into the hands of the user rather than relying on automated restrictions. By shifting the focus to manual entry, this tool introduces just enough pause to help you break bad habits and build a healthier routine.

Our main goal is to provide a clean, straightforward, and private space for anyone looking to improve their focus and digital balance. Because you enter your own data, your personal device activity is never tracked, stored, or shared behind the scenes. Whether you are a student trying to stay on top of coursework or someone simply looking to spend less time staring at a screen, this platform is designed to support your personal goals. We believe that the best way to manage your screen time is to take ownership of it, one honest log at a time.
